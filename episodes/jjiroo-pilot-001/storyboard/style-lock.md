# jjiroo-pilot-001 Style Lock

## Style Basis

- Use the uploaded stills in `generated_scenes/` as the canonical visual basis.
- Preserve the exact original uploaded drawing style. Do not reinterpret into a prettier, more realistic, more cinematic, or more painterly style.
- Horizontal 16:9 storytelling frame.
- Hand-drawn pastel 2D illustration.
- Rough grainy black pencil outline.
- Soft flat low-saturation pastel colors.
- Non-photorealistic, non-3D, storybook finish.
- Bright daytime Tancheon riverside atmosphere.

## Character Lock

- Jjiroo is the protagonist.
- Jjiroo must stay cream to light beige, with droopy ears, dot eyes, short muzzle, short legs, and a small rounded body.
- Jjiroo must never gain erect ears, extra fur detail, photoreal fur, longer legs, or a different breed silhouette.
- Jjonga is the supporting dog.
- Jjonga must stay medium brown, smaller-framed, more tense and watchful, clearly distinct from Jjiroo in color and silhouette.
- The mother is a simple stylized human guardian. Keep her secondary to Jjiroo in framing except during the rescue beat.

## Continuity Rules

- Keep one Jjiroo only. No duplicate dog, no second version of the same dog, no extra animal copies.
- Keep the same geography from path to duck-rest area to river entry to climb-out bank.
- Scene-n final frame and scene-(n+1) opening frame must begin from the same state before motion continues.
- Keep the anecdote comic and safe, not violent or predatory.

## Forbidden Drift

- text
- subtitles
- speech bubbles
- letters
- numbers
- logos
- watermarks
- photorealism
- 3D
- style drift
- wrong fur color
- wrong ear shape
- duplicate pet
- extra dog
- extra limbs
- wings
- merged anatomy
- injured ducks
- drowning panic
