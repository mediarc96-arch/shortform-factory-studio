# SHO-15 Emotional Story Concepts

## Request

- Source issue: `SHO-15`
- Working title: `자극적인 이야기 10개-test`
- Requested outcome: 10 short-form story concepts built to create immediate curiosity and high completion intent through stakes, conflict, and reveal

## Editorial Intent

- Target viewer: Korean short-form viewers who respond to emotionally intense fiction, interpersonal conflict, injustice, sacrifice, regret, or reversal
- Viewer promise: each concept should make the viewer feel that a hidden truth, betrayal, or emotional payoff is about to be revealed if they stay to the end
- First-5-seconds rule: the opening beat must introduce a clear emotional rupture, unresolved tension, or shocking turn that makes the next reveal feel necessary

## Guardrails

- Do not use fake real-world allegations, deceptive framing, or fabricated public-figure claims
- If a concept touches sensitive or plausibly real events, label it clearly as fiction in the concept notes and downstream script/disclosure planning
- Avoid generic sludge: every concept needs a specific relationship, conflict engine, and end reveal
- Do not move any concept forward unless it can be stated with a single viewer promise and a clean hook logic

## Required Output For Concept Slate

Each candidate concept should include:

1. Working title
2. Target viewer
3. Core emotional trigger
4. First-5-seconds hook
5. Viewer promise
6. Story engine in 2 to 3 sentences
7. End reveal or payoff
8. Fiction / disclosure note
9. Risk note if the angle could feel exploitative, repetitive, or trust-eroding

## Routing

- `Audience Researcher`: demand evidence, audience language, competitor formats, and emotional pattern map
- `Content Strategist`: convert evidence into 10 viable concepts with hook logic and editorial fit
- `Head of Content`: review concepts for quality, trust, and production readiness before any scripting
